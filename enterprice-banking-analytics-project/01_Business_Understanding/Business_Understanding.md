# Phase 1 – Business Understanding

## Project Title

**Enterprise Banking Analytics System**

## Project Background

Banks generate large volumes of data every day through customer registrations, account management, financial transactions, loan processing, and branch operations. However, raw operational data alone does not help management make informed decisions. To improve strategic planning and operational efficiency, this project aims to transform raw banking data into meaningful business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

## Business Problem

The bank stores data across multiple relational tables, including customers, accounts, transactions, loans, branches, and reference tables. Since this information is distributed across different entities, management cannot easily answer important business questions such as customer growth, account performance, branch performance, transaction trends, and loan portfolio health.

## Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to analyze banking operations by integrating multiple relational tables and generating actionable business insights. The project focuses on customer analytics, account analytics, transaction analysis, loan portfolio analysis, and branch performance analysis.

## Stakeholders

* Executive Management
* Branch Managers
* Operations Team
* Loan Department
* Customer Relationship Team

## Business Questions

The project aims to answer questions such as:

* How many customers does the bank have?
* Which customer segments contribute the most?
* Which account types are most popular?
* Which branches process the highest transaction volume?
* What is the total loan portfolio?
* Which loan statuses dominate the portfolio?
* What are the monthly transaction trends?
* Which customers maintain the highest account balances?
* Which branches require operational improvement?

## Success Metrics

The project will be considered successful if it provides:

* Accurate business KPIs
* Reliable SQL reports
* Interactive Power BI dashboards
* Actionable business recommendations
* Clean and structured analytical datasets

## Project Scope

This project covers the complete analytics lifecycle:

* Business Understanding
* Data Understanding
* Data Cleaning
* Database Design
* SQL Business Analysis
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* Power BI Dashboard Development
* Business Insights and Recommendations

## Expected Business Impact

The final solution will enable management to monitor business performance, evaluate customer behavior, analyze transaction activity, assess loan portfolios, compare branch performance, and support data-driven decision-making.
